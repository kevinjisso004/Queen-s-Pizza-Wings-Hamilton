About Our Mozzarella Sticks

There is a narrow window where a mozzarella stick is perfect, and we aim for it every time. Ours come six to an order, breaded and fried until the coating is deep golden and crisp, with the mozzarella inside hot enough to pull into a long stretch on the first bite. Marinara sauce comes on the side for dipping.
