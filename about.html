Our story

Where Hamilton’s love for pizza and wings meets Queen Street South hospitality. From fresh dough and generous wing sauces to late-night cravings, every part of Queen’s Pizza & Wings is built around real comfort food, done right.

How it started

Three chapters, one kitchen

01

A passion for real comfort food

Welcome to Queen’s Pizza & Wings, where we’ve been serving Hamilton’s Queen Street South neighbourhood with honest, made-to-order pizza and wings. Our journey started with a simple idea — fresh dough, generous portions and sauces worth coming back for, no shortcuts, just real comfort food.

02

A menu that became a Hamilton favourite

Our hand-stretched pizzas sit beside crowd-favourite chicken wings, panzerotti, poutine and garlic bread. Every order is built around value-priced specials and customizable topping combinations, so there’s always a combo that fits your table — and your budget.

03

More than meals

We believe great pizza and wings shouldn’t stop when the sun goes down. With late-night hours running until 3 AM, seven days a week, we’re here for game nights, movie nights and everything in between — dine-in, takeout, delivery or catering.

What we promise

Why people stay loyal

Locally Run, Hamilton Proud

Queen’s Pizza & Wings is part of a small, locally run Hamilton group built around affordable pizza, wings and honest service — not a franchise, just neighbours feeding neighbours.

Open Late, Every Single Day

Cravings don’t watch the clock. We’re open daily from 11 AM to 3 AM, so pizza and wings are always within reach — whether it’s lunch, dinner or a middle-of-the-night run.

Fast Pickup, Delivery & Dine-In

Takeout, delivery, dine-in or catering for your next event — however you want your pizza and wings, we make it quick, convenient and easy on the wallet.
